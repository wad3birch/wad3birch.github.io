III

Research

I make LLM agents dependable: diagnosing how they fail, keeping them aligned as capabilities and deployments shift, and measuring whether they can be trusted.

01 Agent Reliability

Why do LLM agents break down over multi-turn interaction — and how can we diagnose where?

I study how LLM agents fail across multi-turn interaction, input faults, and cooperative settings, how those failures can be diagnosed, and how execution experience can make agent harnesses more adaptive.

  • MemoHarness arXiv'26
  • Drift-Bench ICML'26
  • IntraAI WWW'26 Demo

02 Robust Alignment

Where does alignment stop holding as models gain new capabilities or move to the edge?

I investigate how alignment risks emerge as models acquire new capabilities or move into constrained deployment environments, together with guardian and advisor mechanisms for safer behavior.

  • Alignment Risks from Capability-Seeking RL Training ICML'26
  • Guardian-as-an-Advisor ACL'26 Findings
  • AI Alignment Breaks at the Edge Position Paper

03 Trustworthy Evaluation

How do we know a foundation model stays reliable where it is actually used?

I develop benchmarks and evaluation protocols that test whether generative, vision-language, and domain-specific foundation models remain reliable in realistic use cases.

  • TrustGen ICLR'26
  • AutoDavis KDD'26 D&B
  • PolicyLLM ACL'26 Findings
